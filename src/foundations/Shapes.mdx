## The rules

- **Cut from the circle.** Every form on that page is the face circle
  intersected, subtracted or repeated. That is the test for a new one.
- **Not all of them are soft.** The four-point star and the cone have points.
  "Rounded and organic, no sharp corners" is a fair description of most of the
  set and a wrong description of the set — check the artwork, not a summary.
- **One approved colour pair per composition.** Yellow↔Orange, Pink↔Purple,
  Green↔Forest, Blue↔Ocean. Page 36 shows the whole palette because it is a
  catalogue; a real composition picks one pair.
- **Solid fill or a photo mask.** No gradients inside a shape, no strokes.
- **Keep internal padding**, so the shape reads as a shape and not as a crop.

## In motion

Two places animate them today, and the student home is the larger:

**Student home** — twenty shapes are not all on screen at once; a handful sit
behind the content at 3–4% opacity under `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, each with
its own duration and a negative delay so they are already mid-cycle on load.
Five motions, all `ease-in-out infinite` except the spin:

**Results hero** — two circles, blurred and drifting, over a shifting gradient
at 8s, 10s and 14s. Three durations, none a multiple of another, so the loop
never visibly repeats. Both carry `blur-3xl`: unblurred, two drifting circles
read as objects moving across the card rather than as atmosphere.

**Both now stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.** Neither did until recently.
The results hero had three unguarded animations and the student home thirteen,
so a learner who had asked their OS to reduce motion got all sixteen. The shapes
stay — they are texture, not movement — they simply hold still.

## Where this lives, and what is missing

The twenty vectors live in this package, at `assets/shapes/`, and that is the
only committed copy — consuming apps that need them in `public/` copy them from
here at build time (a sync script on `predev`/`prebuild`, with the app copy
gitignored) so the two sources cannot drift. Change a shape here and every app
picks it up on its next build.
- **The animations belong to their pages**, not to this package: the student
  home defines its five keyframes inline and the results hero has its three in
  `globals.css`. Nothing here can enforce either, and a third surface wanting a
  drifting-shape background would write a fourth copy.
- **Nothing checks any of this.** The radius guard covers controls; a decorative
  shape is outside that scale by design and is neither checked nor checkable.
  This page is a convention, not a guarantee — unlike most of what sits beside it.
