### The Accessibility panel

Below the canvas, next to `Controls` / `Actions` / `Interactions`, there's an
**Accessibility** tab. It runs a real axe-core audit on every story
automatically — no button to press. It has two sub-tabs:

- **Violations** — real issues (e.g. insufficient color contrast), each
  expandable to the exact element and CSS selector, with a "Jump to element"
  button that scrolls/highlights it on the canvas.
- **Passes** — checks that already succeed, for reference.

This isn't just for show — it already caught a real bug: `Avatar`'s `level`
ribbon colors initially failed WCAG AA contrast against their white text,
found and fixed within minutes of turning the addon on.
